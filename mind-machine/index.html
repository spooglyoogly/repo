Flashing light warning

This app flashes light at 0.5–40 Hz. Flicker in the 3–30 Hz range can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy, sometimes with no prior history.

  • Don't use it if you or close family have epilepsy or seizures.
  • Stop at once if you feel dizzy, nauseous, or notice twitching or vision changes.
  • Don't use it while driving or operating anything.

Press Stop, Esc, or tap the screen to end a session at any time.
